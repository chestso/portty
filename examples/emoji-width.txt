Variation selectors — VS16 widens to 2 cells, VS15 narrows to 1 cell:

Group A — text-default (bare=1 cell, VS16=2 cells):
  bare:  |⚠|☀|☁|☘|⛏|
  ref:   |A|B|C|D|E|
  VS16:  |⚠️|☀️|☁️|☘️|⛏️|
  ref:   |AB|CD|EF|GH|IJ|

Group B — emoji-default (bare=2 cells); VS15 narrows to 1 cell, rendering from emoji font if available:
  bare:  |⚡|⚽|⛄|⛔|⛽|
  ref:   |AB|CD|EF|GH|IJ|
  VS15:  |⚡︎|⚽︎|⛄︎|⛔︎|⛽︎|
  ref:   |A|B|C|D|E|
